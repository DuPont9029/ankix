# ADH and water balance

- ADH (vasopressin) is made in the hypothalamus and released by the posterior pituitary.
- The main trigger is a rise in plasma osmolarity; low blood volume also increases its release.
- It acts on V2 receptors in the collecting duct and inserts aquaporin-2 into the apical membrane.
- Without ADH, large volumes of dilute urine are produced (diabetes insipidus).
